Small-business website

Hippity Hoppity Clean

Problem
A local cleaning company needed a public presence that felt warm, trustworthy, and easy for customers to use on a phone.
Build

02

Custom estimating software

Quote Goat

Problem
Flooring estimates depended on practical knowledge held in one contractor’s head, including labor rates, waste factors, difficult access, room conditions, and material-specific judgment.
Build
Sovereign Flame translated that experience into a structured quoting engine with multi-room support, contractor-specific overrides, waste calculations, access surcharges, and pricing logic derived from real field practice.
Result
Informal contractor judgment became reusable software that can produce more consistent, explainable estimates.

The goat may scream. The mathematics remains composed.
